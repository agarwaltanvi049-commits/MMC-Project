Western Pop Music

Western Pop Music is modern, catchy, and globally popular. It focuses on rhythm, emotion, and digital production.

It evolved from jazz, rock, and R&B and is now one of the biggest global music industries.

Indian Classical Music

Indian Classical Music is one of the oldest music systems in the world. It is based on Ragas and Talas.

It is deeply spiritual and allows improvisation, making every performance unique.

Indian classical music is emotion expressed through sound.

Fusion Music (Coke Studio Style)

Fusion music blends traditional and modern styles together. It creates a bridge between old and new musical cultures.

Platforms like Coke Studio help preserve folk traditions while making them modern and relatable.
